Veil

Private by default for the wallets and dapps you already use.

One route per account over Tor, so the providers your apps talk to can't see your IP or tell your accounts apart. When a provider blocks Tor, Veil re-routes around it - and never falls back to a direct connection without asking you.

Protection is on
Dapp reads leave through Tor, one route per account.

What this hides - and what it doesn't

We keep this honest. Privacy you don't actually have is worse than none.

Hidden from providers
  • Your IP addresson every dapp read (and wallet traffic too, with the desktop helper).
  • That your accounts belong togetherfor RPC traffic - each account gets its own route, so providers see strangers.
Still visible
  • Which addresses you look upproviders still see the addresses you query (that needs PIR - a different project).
  • Your wallet's own multi-account APIse.g. MetaMask's balance service still takes all your accounts in one request.
  • That you use Torthe Tor gateway sees you use it - not what for.
No accounts. No telemetry. Network code is sandboxed and hash-pinned. Protection is on
