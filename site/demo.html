Watch your IP disappear

This page makes the read a dapp makes, for two of your accounts: a JSON-RPC request to a server that reports back the IP it saw. It is the same read either way. With Veil on, each read is routed over Tor on its own circuit, so the server sees a different exit per account. With Veil off, both reads carry your real IP, linking your accounts to one person. This page is where you point the extension.

Needs the Veil extension installed and on (and, on this build, your local gateway running). With Veil off or not installed, both accounts show your real IP, that is the leak; turn Veil on and each read exits at its own Tor IP. The reads go to an IP echo (demo-echo/), overridable with ?echo=<url>. Addresses queried are still visible to providers by design; Veil removes your IP and the link between your accounts, not the addresses.  Back to Veil
