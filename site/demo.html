What your RPC provider sees

Every dapp reads your balances from an RPC provider, and each read hands that provider your IP and the address. Below is the read, and the provider's log, with Veil off and on. This page is where you point the extension.

Checking for the Veil extension…
  1. Install Veil and load it in Chrome.Download
  2. Turn it on, click the Veil toolbar icon until it reads “Private · Tor”.
  3. Run the reads below.

These logs are real, not mocked: the reads actually go out and a server reports back the IP it saw. Without Veil, a provider logs your real IP for both accounts, tying them to one person; with Veil, each account arrives from its own Tor exit. What you look up stays visible by design, Veil hides your IP and the link between your accounts, not which addresses you query.  Back to Veil
